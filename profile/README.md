# Iman Ahmadi Naeeni

I am a PhD student in Economics at **Georgia State University**, in the Andrew Young School of Policy Studies.

My research interests are **energy economics, electricity markets, and banking**. I study the relationships between electricity demand, carbon policy, financial conditions, and credit allocation.

I work with **Python and Stata** to build research datasets, estimate panel models, and assess the sensitivity of empirical results.

### Selected research code

- [Data centers and electricity markets](https://github.com/ImanAhmadin96/energy-market/tree/main/projects/data-centers-electricity): hourly panels and electricity-price regressions.
- [Bank lending and carbon intensity](https://github.com/ImanAhmadin96/energy-market/tree/main/projects/climate-credit): syndicated-loan panels and emissions matching. Joint work with Givi Melkadze and Giseong Kim.
- [Financing the electricity sector](https://github.com/ImanAhmadin96/energy-market/tree/main/projects/electricity-finance): loan-facility measurement and classification.
- [Carbon costs and electricity offers](https://github.com/ImanAhmadin96/energy-market/tree/main/projects/carbon-pass-through): offer-price analysis and fixed-effect estimation.

These repositories and project folders contain selected workflows from ongoing research. Data requirements, methodological limitations, and reproduction instructions are documented alongside the code.
